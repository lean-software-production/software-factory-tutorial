## What this costs

Run the line again and watch the terminal.

The readable output is gone. Where the doer used to explain what it was changing, JSON scrolls past.
Your terminal is now showing you strictly more information than it did yesterday, and you can follow
strictly less of it.

That is not an accident of formatting, and the fix is not to pick a prettier mode. A record and a
view are two different artefacts for two different readers, and the line has just traded one for
the other. The run you would most want to follow — the one going wrong right now — is the one
you can no longer watch.

Nothing is being taken away that the next lesson does not give back. The point is that you had to
give it up to get a record, and that getting it back is a separate job.
