This workbook builds a software factory one agent at a time. You run each step in your own terminal;
the page shows the command, tells you what to look for, and records what you observed. It never runs
your terminal for you.

Part 1 builds one agent at a time and runs everything by hand. Part 2 joins those agents into an
assembly line and takes the human out of it. Underneath every lesson is the question a factory has to
answer for itself: **who checks the change?**
