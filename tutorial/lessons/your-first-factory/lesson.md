# Your first factory

Build a tiny throwaway factory so you can feel the loop before we study it carefully.

This is deliberately quick and crude. You will write a small spec, a worker prompt, and a script
that runs exactly two Pi passes. That is enough to watch work move through a loop without
pretending this is a production factory.
